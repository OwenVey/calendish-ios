# Automatic household onboarding and transferable ownership

A new account gets a solo household automatically unless it arrives through an invitation, in which case it joins the invited household directly after sign-up. Onboarding asks for the person's name without requiring a create-or-join decision; household naming and invitations are available later, and Settings supports joining another household. This avoids making first-time users choose a household arrangement before they understand the app.

An owner can manually transfer ownership to another current member. If the owner leaves without doing so, ownership transfers automatically to the longest-standing remaining member, identified in the departure confirmation. If nobody remains, the empty household and its groceries, meal plans, and household-owned recipes are deleted after the departing person is informed; personal recipes survive.

Leaving or being removed automatically gives the person a fresh solo household with an empty grocery list and meal plan. Their personal recipe collection remains available, and they do not repeat onboarding.

Invitations survive sign-up. An invitation opened by someone already in a household requires a move confirmation showing the destination, which data follows or stays, any ownership transfer, and any empty-household deletion. The invitation is validated before any membership or data changes; canceling or a failed join leaves the current household intact. An invitation to the person's current household simply opens that household.
