# Calendish

Calendish connects recipes, planned meals, and household groceries.

## Language

**Household**:
A group of people who share a grocery list and meal plan in Calendish.

**Personal recipe**:
A recipe owned by a person that remains in their collection when they join or leave a household.

**Shared recipe**:
A personal recipe made available to its owner's household. Sharing gives household members access to the same recipe rather than a separate copy.

**Household recipe**:
A recipe owned by a household, independent of any person's personal recipe collection.

**Planned meal**:
A meal scheduled in a household's meal plan with a saved recipe version and serving count.

**Grocery import review**:
The choice of recipe ingredients and serving counts to add to the household grocery list, entered from a recipe or planned meals.

**On List**:
A reviewed ingredient whose food name matches an unchecked grocery attributed to the same recipe title, regardless of quantity.

**Selected ingredient**:
A reviewed ingredient the person has chosen to add to groceries. Existing groceries inform the initial choice; later grocery changes do not override that choice.
